Port Whitelist

Ports a LAN client may reach

Only these ports can be reached on the internet. Everything else — including anything trying to reach this bridge from the internet — is blocked. An entry is a port number, optionally suffixed /tcp or /udp; a bare number means TCP. The defaults cover NTRIP (2101, 2102) and RustDesk (21115–21117, including 21116/udp, which RustDesk does not work without).

Two things are always allowed and do not belong on this list: DNS, to the resolver the upstream network hands out, and NTP (123/udp) to any server. NTP is open for devices on the LAN as well as for the bridge itself — a tablet with the wrong clock fails TLS and NTRIP authentication, and this bridge is its only way out. Adding 123/udp here would change nothing and would spend one of your twelve rules.

Saving takes effect immediately — this list is the one WAN setting that does not restart the bridge. The list has no effect at all while the WAN is switched off, since nothing is being routed out.
